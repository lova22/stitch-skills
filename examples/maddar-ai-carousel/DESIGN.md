# Maddar AI — Social Carousel Design System

## Format
Instagram/LinkedIn carousel slide, fixed 1080 x 1350 px (4:5 portrait), Arabic RTL (Egyptian dialect), `dir="rtl"`.

## Brand
- Logo: "Maddar AI" wordmark (black "Maddar", green "AI") with a circular orbit/target icon (thin green circle, dashed outer ring, crosshair lines, nodes) — top-left.
- Page counter "01 / 08" top-right in green bold.
- Footer: "maddar.ai" bottom-left, progress indicator bottom-right (8 short dashes, active ones green, rest light grey).
- Thin hairline divider under the header.

## Color
- Primary green (mint/emerald): #1FC28C
- Light green tint: #C9F0DF
- Ink black: #111111
- Paper off-white background: #F4F3EF with a faint light-grey graph-paper grid
- Card white/paper: #F7F6F2
- Dark card: #0E0E0E with white text

## Typography
- Arabic headlines: "Alexandria" or "Cairo" weight 900, very large, tight leading, black.
- Arabic body: "Cairo" / "Tajawal" weight 600–700.
- Latin labels: "Inter" bold.
- Highlighted words: green marker-highlight band behind the text, or hand-drawn green underline stroke.

## Style
Tactile collage / cut-paper aesthetic: torn paper notes, washi tape strips, binder clips, halftone black-and-white photo cutouts, crumpled paper speech bubbles, hand-drawn green doodle strokes (burst lines, squiggles, arrows). Flat, no gradients, no glossy shadows except subtle paper drop shadows.
