# Keyword plan for crochetpatternsco.com: US first draft (2026-10-15)

Source: two Semrush Keyword Magic Tool exports, US database, seed "crochet patterns", volume 50 or more per month.
- `...us_2026-10-15.csv`: 16,963 keywords, no difficulty filter.
- `...us_2026-10-15_1.csv`: 16,701 keywords with KD 49 or lower (262 fewer, so almost everything in this niche is KD 49 or lower).
Total monthly searches in the file are about 4.15 million, but that counts many near-identical variants (for example "crochet hat pattern", "crochet hat patterns"), so it is not real distinct demand.

## What the data says
1. The niche is easy by Semrush KD. Most pattern keywords are KD 10 to 30. KD is a guide, not a promise: a new domain still needs links and time.
2. Demand is by item (hat, blanket, cardigan, scarf, bunny), plus "free" and "beginner" modifiers. "Baby bunny amigurumi crochet pattern" is not in the file (under 50 searches), so the two pattern posts target a phrase nobody searches.
3. The biggest single keyword is "crochet patterns for beginners": 135,000 a month, KD 29.
4. Spanish and UK words show up: "ganchillo patterns" 33,100, "crochet jersey patterns" 8,100 at KD 8 ("jersey" is the UK word for sweater).
5. This export is patterns only. Guide topics (hook size chart, yarn weight chart, gauge swatch, hat size chart) were not in the seed, so they need their own export.

## Order to publish from the 1,000 PDF library
The site has 2 patterns, so the keyword list should decide which PDFs go live first. Highest demand at low difficulty, in this order:
1. Baby / newborn blankets
2. Hats and beanies
3. Cardigans and sweaters
4. Scarves
5. Crochet flowers and granny squares
6. Bunnies and other animals (one animal page per animal: cat, duck, frog, octopus, dragon each 2,400 to 2,900)
7. Christmas ornaments and snowflakes (seasonal: publish by early November)
Publish only patterns you have the rights to. Use the searched wording in the title, such as "Crochet Hat Pattern" rather than a name only.

## 12-week plan
- Weeks 1-2: apply the quick fixes; write intros for the beginner, blanket and hat hubs; request indexing in Search Console.
- Weeks 3-6: publish 3 patterns a week in the order above, each with the focus keyword in title, slug, first sentence and an H2 (Rank Math recipe).
- Weeks 7-12: roundup pages ("10 free crochet baby blanket patterns") linking to patterns; Christmas content before the season; translate the top 5 patterns into Spanish.

What they show:
- **Traffic comes from a few evergreen pages.** daisyfarmcrafts: its #1 keyword "crochet newborn blanket patterns" (22,200 searches, position 1) is 10.4% of its traffic, and its top page (a baby blanket) is 12.5%; others are an easy beanie page (606 keywords), "42 free basic crochet stitches", and beginner scarves. makeanddocrew: home page 10.4%, a hexagon sweater pattern 5.3%, "easy free crochet sweater patterns" 4.9%, "crochet borders" 4.3%. mamainastitch: blanket and stitch tutorials ("crochet blanket", "how to crochet a blanket", "moss stitch crochet").
- **Country split of their keywords:** US about 52 to 54%, UK 9 to 10%, Australia about 10%, Canada 8 to 9%, mobile-US about 5%. This matches our plan: the four English markets are about 80% of demand.
- **English works in Europe:** daisyfarmcrafts ranks #9 in France for "crochet patterns for beginners" (5,400 searches there) and that single keyword is 18.9% of its French traffic.
- **They are old and big:** thousands of referring domains and 13K to 56K backlinks. Our site has almost none, so links are the main gap, not only content.
- **Traffic is flat or falling** for two of the three, so the niche is mature; the one growing site (daisyfarmcrafts) is adding keywords through many long-tail pattern pages.

What we do with it:
1. Build pillar pages first, matching the winners: newborn/baby blanket patterns hub, free sweater and cardigan patterns, hat/beanie patterns, a crochet stitches library ("free basic crochet stitches" style), plus the beginner hub. Fill each with real patterns from the PDF library plus a roundup page that links to them.
2. Target positions 1 to 3 on long-tail pattern keywords (KD under 30), not head terms such as "crochet".
3. Start a link plan (Pinterest and Reddit sharing, craft resource lists, free charts others link to, partner blogs). Backlink Gap export in the next round.
4. Expect a slow start: these sites took years. A realistic first-year goal is hundreds to low thousands of visits a month; no ranking guarantee.
